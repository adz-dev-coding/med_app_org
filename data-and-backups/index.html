Data & backups

Your records stay local unless you choose otherwise

Lifestyle Tracking uses a local database for custom items and entry history. Export, import, reminders and Android backup are separate user- or platform-controlled paths.

Local Room database

The app stores custom items, categories, saved amounts, entry records, timestamps and related state in the app's local Android storage.

No developer cloud account

Lifestyle Tracking does not require an account and does not use an Adz Dev Coding cloud database for your personal journal.

Local diagnostics

Bounded troubleshooting events may be kept locally. They are not automatically transmitted to the developer.

Optional reminders

Android notification and alarm access may be used to display reminders you configure on the device.

Export and import options

CSV export
Creates a spreadsheet-friendly copy of entry history. On supported Android versions it may save directly to Documents, with the system picker used as a fallback.
JSON backup
Creates a structured backup through the Android document picker. You choose the destination.
SQLite backup
Creates a raw copy of the app database for external analysis. This file may contain the complete local journal.
JSON import
Reads a backup selected through Android and replaces the current local journal when the import succeeds.

Exports may contain personal information

CSV, JSON and SQLite files can include item names, categories, descriptors, amounts, units and entry dates or times. Do not publish or casually share them. Protect them according to the sensitivity of your journal.

Android and third-party providers set their own boundaries

  • The Android document picker may offer local storage or third-party providers installed on your device.
  • A cloud provider receives an exported file only when you choose that provider as the destination.
  • Android backup or device transfer may copy app data according to your platform and account settings.
  • Adz Dev Coding does not operate those storage or backup services.

Your controls

  • Review the destination before exporting a backup.
  • Keep independent copies only where you trust the storage location.
  • Delete exported files separately when they are no longer needed.
  • Review notification and alarm permissions in Android settings.
  • Review Android backup and device-transfer settings for your account and device.
  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove local app storage, subject to platform backup and restore behaviour.
