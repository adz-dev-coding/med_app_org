Clear boundaries

Flexible, private and under your control

The app is designed around a personal local journal rather than accounts, advertising or developer-operated cloud storage.

Local records

Tracked items, categories, saved amounts, timestamps, reminders, settings and local diagnostics are stored on the device.

User-controlled exports

You can export CSV, JSON or SQLite files and choose where Android saves them. JSON import is available for restoring a backup.

No account or advertising

The app does not require registration and is not designed around advertising, developer telemetry or an online personal-data profile.

What it does

One place for everyday tracking

  • Create custom items with names, categories, icons, amounts and units.
  • Use quick suggestions for common vitamins, supplements and minerals.
  • Pin frequently used items to Today and record them with a tap.
  • Review and manage previous entries in the History Log.
  • Explore totals and trends across selected time ranges.
  • Set optional local reminders through Android notifications.

Your entries, your meaning

Lifestyle Tracking records the information you choose to enter. It does not interpret your data, score your choices or tell you what actions to take.

This website
