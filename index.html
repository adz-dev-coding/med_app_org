Clear boundaries

Private, useful, and under your control

The app is designed around a local journal rather than accounts, advertising, or developer-operated cloud storage.

Local journal

Medication entries, saved amounts, intake history, settings, and local diagnostics are stored on the device.

User-controlled exports

You can export CSV, JSON, or SQLite files and choose where Android saves them. JSON import is available for restoring a backup.

No telemetry

The app does not require an account and is not designed to send medication records, analytics, advertising data, or local diagnostics to Adz Dev Coding.

What it does

A factual medication journal

  • Maintain a medication catalogue and optional saved amounts.
  • Record medication, amount, unit, date, and time.
  • Review and manage previous intake records.
  • View totals and trends across selected time ranges.
  • Use optional reminders through Android notifications and alarm scheduling.

No medical interpretation

Medication App records information you enter. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend medication, determine dosage, judge adherence, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
