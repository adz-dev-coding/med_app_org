Lifestyle Tracking Privacy Policy

How the Lifestyle Tracking Android application handles information when you use its personal tracking features.

Publisher
Adz Dev Coding
Effective
29 July 2026
Contact
privacy@medication-app.pl

Scope

This policy applies to Lifestyle Tracking for Android. The app is designed as a private, local-first tool for recording user-defined daily items and does not require a Lifestyle Tracking account.

Information stored by the app

Depending on the features you use, the app may store item names and descriptors, categories, icons, saved amount presets, recorded amounts and units, local dates and times, reminder settings, app preferences and local diagnostic events. These records are stored in the app's local storage on your Android device.

Lifestyle Tracking records information you choose to enter. It does not determine the meaning or quality of your entries.

No developer telemetry or advertising

Lifestyle Tracking is not designed to send your personal records, entry history, local diagnostic events, analytics, advertising identifiers or usage telemetry to Adz Dev Coding. The app does not require registration, login, a developer-operated personal-data account or advertising.

The current Android manifest does not declare general Internet access for the app's tracking workflows. If future functionality changes this boundary, this policy will be updated before that functionality is represented as part of the public app.

Local diagnostics

The app may keep a small, bounded local record of events such as screen changes, dialog actions, success or failure states, session start or background events and unclean exits. These diagnostics are stored on the device for local troubleshooting and are not automatically sent to Adz Dev Coding.

Exports and imports

You may choose to export tracking history as CSV, export a JSON backup or export the local SQLite database. You choose the destination through Android or, where supported, a local Documents location. A destination may be local storage or a third-party document provider selected by you.

Once an exported file leaves the app's private storage, it is controlled by the destination you selected and that provider's terms. Exported files may contain personal lifestyle records, categories, amounts, units and timestamps. Store and share them carefully.

JSON import replaces the current local journal with the data contained in the selected backup after the app processes the file. Lifestyle Tracking does not upload the imported file to Adz Dev Coding.

Notifications and reminders

The app may request notification permission and alarm access for optional reminders. These permissions allow Android to display reminders on your device. They do not give Adz Dev Coding access to your tracking records.

You can review or revoke notification and alarm permissions in Android settings. Some reminder behaviour may stop if the permissions are not granted.

Android backup and device transfer

The application currently allows Android backup and uses Android's backup and data-extraction framework. Depending on your device, Android version, account settings and manufacturer services, Android may back up or transfer app data. These platform operations are controlled by Android and the relevant service provider, not by Adz Dev Coding.

To reduce retention through those services, review your Android backup and device-transfer settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not necessarily remove copies already held by a platform backup or an exported file you created.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you delete them through app behaviour, clear the app's data or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup and restore behaviour. Exported CSV, JSON and SQLite files must be deleted separately from the location where you saved them.

Local diagnostic records may be cleared by app behaviour. Clearing diagnostics does not delete your tracked items or history.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer the request and retain it only as reasonably necessary for support, security, recordkeeping and applicable legal obligations.

Do not email databases, backup files, detailed personal histories, credentials or unrelated private documents unless they are genuinely required and you understand what they contain.

Age, purpose and policy changes

Lifestyle Tracking is a general personal recordkeeping tool. It does not interpret your entries, score your choices or provide professional guidance.

We may update this policy when the app, permissions, platform requirements or data practices change. The current version will remain at this URL with an updated effective date.
